# Academy motion pages

This section rebuilds official Motion examples as Academy page compositions. Each story is a page, not a mechanic demo.

The runtime is `motion` **13.2.0** only. Licence MIT. Docs: https://motion.dev/docs/react . Source: https://github.com/motiondivision/motion . React `^18 || ^19`.

Issue 231 supplies colors, Brand Sans, Brand Script, and the live-text wordmark. These pages consume those tokens. They do not invent new colors or faces. The Article body uses `@tailwindcss/typography` 0.5.20 with `prose prose-academy`. Long-form proof lives under Typography.

Photographs stay in full color. `filter` is `none`. `mix-blend-mode` is `normal`. The files live in `design-system/public/photos/`. `community-run.jpg` records copyright Hanna Lassen in EXIF.

Reduced motion shows the final state at once. `MotionConfig reducedMotion="user"` is the default. The Reduced motion stories set `always`.
